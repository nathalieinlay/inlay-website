Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

inlay is an app for making edits of products you love. This policy explains what information inlay uses, why, and the choices you have. inlay is run by Nathalie Davies, based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your information (the "data controller"). Contact: hello@inlay.info.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored encrypted; we can never see it).
  • Your name, username, bio and profile picture, if you add them.

What you make and do

  • Your edits: their titles, descriptions, tags, backgrounds, stickers and layout.
  • Products you add: name, shop, price, link and the product picture (including stickers you make from your photos).
  • Private notes on products. These are only ever visible to you.
  • Who you follow and block, your likes, your comments, and any reports you send.

Purchases

inlay Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment; we never receive your card details. We only learn whether your subscription is active.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising trackers, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.

How inlay works on your phone

  • Photos: inlay only sees photos you choose. Removing backgrounds happens on your iPhone.
  • Shop links: to read a product's picture, name and price, and to keep prices and stock up to date, inlay opens the shop's page from your iPhone, as a web browser would. The shop may see that request as it would any visit.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your edits, profile, likes and comments (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To keep inlay safe, including reviewing reports and acting on misuse (our legitimate interest in a safe community).
  • To provide Pro features you've paid for (to fulfil our agreement with you).

Who can see what

  • Your name, username, bio and profile picture are visible to everyone on inlay.
  • Public, published edits (with their products, likes and comments) are visible to everyone on inlay. Private edits are visible only to you.
  • Your notes, your blocks and the reports you send are visible only to you (and reports to us).

Where it's stored

inlay stores your information with Supabase, our database and storage provider, which processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Where information is stored or processed outside the UK, it is protected by approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, edits, photos, likes, comments, follows and notes are deleted. Backups are cleared on a rolling basis within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, download or delete your information, and object to or restrict how we use it. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app at any time. For anything else, email hello@inlay.info. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

inlay is for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
