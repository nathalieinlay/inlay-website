Common questions

How do I add a product?

Open an edit and tap +. Paste a link from any shop, or upload a photo. inlay finds the product picture, name, shop and price, and lifts the product off its background.

The background wasn't removed properly

Background removal works best on photos of the product on its own. If a shop link picks a photo with a model, tap a different photo in the row of choices before adding it.

Why has a price changed, or why does it say sold out?

inlay checks each product's shop page now and then and updates the price and stock to match the shop. Some shops don't share this, so a price may occasionally be out of date. Always check the shop before buying.

Who can see my edits?

New edits are private. When you make an edit public and publish it, anyone on inlay can see it. Your notes on products are always private to you.

How do I cancel inlay Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose inlay and cancel. Cancel at least a day before your trial or billing period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or edit and tap … at the top, or press and hold a comment. Reports are private and are never shown to the person you report.

How do I delete my account?

In inlay, go to My profile, tap the settings cog, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, edits, photos, likes and comments. If you have Pro, cancel it with Apple as well, because deleting your account doesn't stop Apple billing you.
