Our thinking

Buy less, choose well

Most shopping is designed to be fast: one tap, next-day delivery, a timer counting down. inlay is designed to slow the decision down.

A pause between wanting and buying

When something catches your eye, you add it to an edit instead of a basket. It waits there. A day or a week later, you look again with fresh eyes, and the things you still love stand out from the things you only wanted for a moment.

Seeing it all together

An edit shows pieces side by side, the way they’d live together in your wardrobe or your home. It becomes obvious when three things do the same job, or when one piece would go with everything you already own. Choosing one good thing over three almost-right ones is the most sustainable decision there is.

Second-hand first

inlay has built-in support for Vinted, eBay and Depop listings. Paste a link and the piece lands on your edit with its photos and price, right next to anything new, and inlay keeps checking it, marking it sold out the moment it sells. We encourage shopping from platforms that focus on second-hand: buying pre-loved is one of the simplest, most sustainable routes into something you love, and often the thing you were looking for already exists.

Gifts people actually want

Share a wishlist edit and the people buying for you know exactly what you’d love, with sizes and notes. Fewer unwanted presents, fewer returns.

No pressure

inlay doesn’t show you adverts, flash sales or countdown timers. Paid partnerships between shops and the people who make edits are always clearly labelled AD. Buying is never the point of inlay; it’s simply there for when you’re sure.

Help

Support

We’re happy to help. Email hello@inlay.info and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

How do I add a product?

Open an edit and tap +. Paste a link from any shop, or upload a photo. inlay finds the product picture, name, shop and price, and lifts the product off its background.

The background wasn’t removed properly

Choose a different photo from the row of choices, or tap Touch up: brush over anything that should go, or use Frame item to draw a box around just the product.

Why has a price changed, or why does it say sold out?

inlay checks each product’s shop page now and then and updates the price and stock to match. Some shops don’t share this, so a price may occasionally be out of date. Always check the shop before buying.

Who can see my edits?

New edits are private. When you make an edit public and publish it, anyone on inlay can see it. Your notes on products are always private to you.

How do I cancel inlay Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose inlay and cancel. Cancel at least a day before your trial or billing period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or edit and tap … at the top, or press and hold a comment. Reports are private and are never shown to the person you report.

How do I delete my account?

In inlay, go to My profile, tap the settings cog, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, edits, photos, likes and comments. If you have Pro, cancel it with Apple as well, because deleting your account doesn’t stop Apple billing you.
